About

Economist by training, builder by habit.

I read the economy through data — and I like building the tools that make that reading faster, clearer and reproducible.

Background

I'm a graduate in Economics and International Business from Universidad Peruana de Ciencias Aplicadas (UPC), in the top third of my class, with 1.5+ years of experience in Financial Planning & Analysis (FP&A) and budget control across the private and public sectors.

Today I work in FP&A at Laureate Perú, and previously in planning and budget at a public-sector institution — environments where good decisions depend on turning scattered numbers into a clear, honest picture. That pushed me toward financial modeling, P&L forecasting, variance analysis and the automation of data workflows (ETL, Power Query, Excel/Power BI).

Alongside finance, I keep a research practice: econometrics, microdata and, increasingly, machine learning and AI as tools for applied economic questions.

Numbers don't speak for themselves — the job is to make them say something true.
